# Regional Content Design Blueprint

This blueprint populates the Universal Gameplay Architecture with region-specific content for the initial regions of **Cebu** and **Ilocos**. While the core mechanics and structure remain identical across LUMINANG, the cultural narrative, language, and characters change to reflect the unique identity of each region.

---

## 1. Ilocos Region

### 1.1 Regional Identity
- **Culture & History:** Strongly tied to heritage, ancestral homes, and craftsmanship.
- **Landmarks:** Calle Crisologo (cobblestone streets, ancestral houses).
- **Traditions:** Abel weaving, traditional pottery (burnay), garlic trading.
- **Atmosphere:** Nostalgic, warm, and deeply rooted in community values.

### 1.2 Regional Guide
- **Name:** Kalaw
- **Role:** Kalaw acts as the player's mentor. He introduces the region, explains Ilokano heritage, guides the STT practice sessions, assigns lessons, and leads the final Crystal Resonance Trial.

### 1.3 Existing NPCs Integration
- **Apo Lakay:** The elder of the community, holds deep historical knowledge; central to quests involving respect and history.
- **Lola Nida:** An expert Abel weaver; quests involve finding materials or discussing her craft.
- **Aling Riza & Mang Lance:** Local merchants; interact during food or market-based language lessons.
- **Tomas, Klara, Lito, Tala:** Community members who provide opportunities for daily conversation and STT practice.

### 1.4 Lesson Content & Story Quests
- **Category 1: Greetings & Respect**
  - **Vocabulary:** Naimbag a bigat (Good morning), Kablaaw (Greetings).
  - **Quest:** "The Morning Rounds." Kalaw tasks the player to greet Apo Lakay and Lola Nida using proper Ilokano terms.
- **Category 2: Food & Trade**
  - **Vocabulary:** Mangan (Eat), Naimas (Delicious), Magkano (How much - *Mano daytoy*).
  - **Quest:** "The Market Run." Purchase ingredients for a local dish from Aling Riza using specific vocabulary.
- **Category 3: Directions & Exploration**
  - **Vocabulary:** Kanan (Right), Kannigid (Left), Diretso (Straight).
  - **Quest:** "Lost Delivery." Help Lito deliver Abel fabric by following Ilokano directional cues.

### 1.5 Minigames & Trials
- **Minigames:** Word Rush using Ilokano vocabulary; Matching Game pairing visual cultural items (Burnay jar, Abel cloth) with their Ilokano names.
- **Crystal Resonance Trial:** "The Heritage Festival." A multi-stage continuous scenario where the player must guide a visitor through Calle Crisologo. The player must greet them properly, explain a local food dish, and give directions to the plaza. Completing this restores the Crystal of Heritage.

### 1.6 Journal
- **Entries:** Ilokano vocabulary, pronunciation guides, cultural notes on Abel weaving and the history of Calle Crisologo.

## 2. Cebu Region

### 2.1 Regional Identity
- **Culture & History:** Vibrant, musical, deeply religious, and oceanic.
- **Landmarks:** Magellan's Cross, Basilica Minore del Santo Niño, coastal areas.
- **Traditions:** Sinulog Festival (dance, music, vibrant colors).
- **Atmosphere:** Energetic, festive, and historically significant.

### 2.2 Regional Guide
- **Name:** *[To be determined]* 
- **Role:** The guide introduces the player to Cebuano history, teaches the language, and prepares the player for the Sinulog celebration.

### 2.3 Existing NPCs Integration
- Roles will center around festival preparation, historical tours, and local cuisine (like Lechon).

### 2.4 Lesson Content & Story Quests
- **Category 1: Greetings & Festival Spirit**
  - **Vocabulary:** Maayong buntag (Good morning), Pit Senyor (Sinulog chant).
  - **Quest:** "The Festival Spirit." Greet the locals preparing for the Sinulog dance.
- **Category 2: Food & Hospitality**
  - **Vocabulary:** Lami (Delicious), Kaon ta (Let's eat).
  - **Quest:** "The Feast." Help a local vendor organize their Lechon stall using Cebuano phrases.
- **Category 3: Directions & History**
  - **Vocabulary:** Tuo (Right), Wala (Left), Layo (Far).
  - **Quest:** "The Historic Path." Guide a lost tourist to Magellan's Cross.

### 2.5 Minigames & Trials
- **Minigames:** Visual matching featuring Sinulog costumes and historical landmarks.
- **Crystal Resonance Trial:** "The Sinulog Preparation." The player must manage the final preparations for the festival—greeting the dancers, sourcing food, and directing the parade. Success restores the Crystal of Devotion.

### 2.6 Journal
- **Entries:** Cebuano vocabulary, notes on the historical significance of Magellan's Cross, and the cultural background of the Sinulog Festival.
